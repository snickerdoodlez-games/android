=== CLASSIC MODE ===
"Find 4 tiles that are related and place them in a row."
→ on Next: auto-solver selects tiles to solve the first row of tiles
"Click on two tiles to swap spots"
→ on Next: auto-solver swaps with matching tiles and completes another row(swap animations play)
"Long press a tile to reveal the defintion of the word in the tile."
→ on Next: long press a tile and show the defintion.  Once the user hits the next button move to the next step
"Your turn!  Find the remaining matches."
→ on Next: tutorial ends (marked seen), user plays freely
