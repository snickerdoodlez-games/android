Word Pairing: Category Match is a sleek, dark-mode word association puzzle game that challenges your brain with 72 handcrafted levels across six distinct game modes. Group related word tiles, crack hidden categories, and watch the neon arcade grid light up as you solve each puzzle.

🎮 SIX UNIQUE GAME MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• CLASSIC — Find the common thread linking 4 word tiles. Simple to learn, endlessly satisfying to solve.
• THEMED — Every category in a level shares a single broad theme. Can you spot what connects them all?
• EMOJI — Words become emojis! Decode visual symbols and group them by hidden meaning.
• MIND MATCH — Timed rounds push your speed and accuracy. 3 rounds, 20 tiles per round, 3 mistakes allowed.
• SYNONYMS — Group words by shared meaning. A vocabulary workout that tests your depth.
• EXPANSION — The grid grows with every stage! Solved tiles lock in place as new rows and columns appear. 3 stages of escalating difficulty.

🧩 HOW TO PLAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Study the word tiles arranged on the neon grid.
2. Tap tiles to select them — look for a hidden category that links 4 words together.
3. Group 4 related tiles and submit your answer.
4. Solve every category on the board to complete the level.
5. Long-press any tile to peek at its definition.
6. Earn stars for fast, mistake-free solves and climb the ranks.

Progress through the 72-level sequence spanning 21 broad categories: Nature, Animals, Astronomy, Food, History, Geography, Religion, Technology, Science, Music, Literature, Movies, Television, Economics, Politics, Lifestyle, Gaming, Cars, Sports, Art, and Mythology.
