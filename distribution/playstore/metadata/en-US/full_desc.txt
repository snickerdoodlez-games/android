Enter the neon-lit world of Word Pairing: Category Match — a sleek, dark-mode word association puzzle that challenges your mind across six unique game modes.

🧩 GAME MODES
• CLASSIC — Group 4 related word tiles into their shared category. Simple to learn, hard to master.
• THEMED — Dive into curated categories spanning Nature, Animals, Food, History, Technology, Science, Music, Movies, Sports, and more.
• EMOJI — Words become emojis! Decode visual clues to find the connecting theme.
• MIND MATCH — Race against the clock in timed matching rounds. 20 tiles, 4 per match, 3 rounds — speed and accuracy are everything.
• SYNONYMS — Group words by meaning. Expand your vocabulary while you play.
• EXPANSION — The grid grows as you solve! Each cleared stage expands the board, with solved tiles locking in place and the challenge scaling up.

🎮 FEATURES
• Dynamic grid layouts that expand, reveal hidden tiles, or filter as you play
